Our Story

Founded in 2021 by two barista friends, Bean & Brew began as a small mobile coffee cart before finding its permanent home on Maple Street. Our mission has always been simple: create a warm, inclusive community space anchored by exceptional coffee.

Core Values

Sustainability

100% of our beans are ethically traded, paying farmers well above fair-trade minimums.

Craftsmanship

Every espresso shot is weighed, timed, and calibrated hourly for peak flavor extraction.

Community

We host local art showcases, open mic nights, and weekly coffee tasting workshops.
